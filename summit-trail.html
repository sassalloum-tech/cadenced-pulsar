Summit Trail

A game from ASCIS for two players (or one) that teaches astronauts to know their own numbers. Climb from square 1 to the summit at 100. Ladders are good readings and habits. Snakes are the slips that cost you ground.

How to play
  1. Two players take turns, Player 1 first. Switch off "Two players" to play alone.
  2. On your turn press Roll the dice. Your token climbs square by square from 1 toward the summit at 100.
  3. Land on the bottom of a ladder to climb: a good reading or habit lifts you up.
  4. Land on the head of a snake to slide down: a missed warning or a risky habit costs ground.
  5. After each ladder or snake, read the short message. It teaches one habit.
  6. You need an exact roll to land on 100. The first player to land on it wins a scratch card with their name on it: scratch it to reveal a treat.
  7. Hover over or tap any square to read a short fact about it. Landing on a plain square shows its fact too.
  8. Reset board starts again. The Sound box switches the sound effects on or off.

It works offline and saves nothing, so it can teach even when the radio is silent.
